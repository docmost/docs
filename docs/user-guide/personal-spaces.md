# Personal Spaces

A personal space is your own space for drafts and work in progress. Nobody else can see it unless you add them. Personal spaces are available in the Business and Enterprise editions.

## Allow personal spaces

Workspace owners and admins can turn on **Allow personal spaces** in **Settings** > **General**.

Turning the setting off stops members from creating new personal spaces. Existing personal spaces aren't changed.

## Create your personal space

1. Click your avatar at the top right and select **Create personal space**.
2. Keep the suggested **Space name** or enter your own.
3. Click **Create**.

Each member can have one personal space. It's added to your favorites. To open it later, click your avatar and select **Personal space**.

## Who can see it

When you create it, you're its only member, with **Full Access**. Workspace owners and admins can't see your personal space or its pages.

To share it, add people or groups in **Space Settings** > **Members**, like in any other [space](/user-guide/spaces#adding-members-to-a-space).

## How it differs from other spaces

A personal space works like any other space. You can rename, export or delete it in **Space Settings**. It can't be archived.

## When a member leaves

Deactivating a member doesn't change their personal space. Deleting a member removes them from it, but the space and its pages stay. If nobody else is a member, nobody can open it. Before you delete someone, ask them to move the pages they want to keep or to add a colleague to the space.
