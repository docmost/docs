# Comments

Comments let people discuss a page without changing its content. You can comment on selected text or on the whole page, reply in threads, and resolve a thread when the discussion is done.

## Add a comment

To comment on part of a page:

1. Select the text.
2. Click **Comment** in the toolbar that appears.
3. Write your comment and click **Save**, or press `Ctrl/Cmd + Enter`.

The text gets highlighted and the comment appears in the comments panel. You can also comment in read mode.

To comment on the page as a whole, open the comments panel and write in the **Add a comment...** box at the bottom.

Comments support formatting, links, emoji, and @mentions of people and pages.

## Find comments

Click the **Comments** icon in the page toolbar to open the comments panel. Clicking highlighted text on the page also opens its comment.

The panel has an **Open** tab and a **Resolved** tab. For a comment on selected text, click the quoted text in the panel to jump to that spot on the page.

## Reply to a comment

Write in the **Reply...** box under a thread and click **Save**. All replies appear in one list under the first comment.

## Resolve a thread

When a discussion is finished, click the check mark at the top of the thread. The thread moves to the **Resolved** tab and the highlight is removed from the text. Anyone who can comment on the page can resolve a thread.

To reopen a thread, find it in the **Resolved** tab and click the check mark again.

## Edit, delete, or share a comment

Open the comment's menu (`...`):

- **Edit comment** changes the text. You can only edit comments you wrote.
- **Delete comment** removes the comment. You can delete your own comments, and space admins can delete any comment. Deleting the first comment of a thread also deletes its replies. Deleted comments can't be restored.
- **Copy link** copies a link that opens the page with the comment shown in the panel.

## Who can comment

Anyone who can edit a page can comment on it. Viewers can comment only if a space admin turns on **Allow viewers to comment** in **Space Settings** > **Security**. This setting is available in the Business and Enterprise editions.

Comments don't appear on publicly shared pages.

## Notifications

You get an in-app notification and an email when:

- someone @mentions you in a comment
- someone comments on a page you watch
- someone replies in a thread you're part of
- someone resolves your comment

Commenting on a page makes you a watcher of it. People you @mention are only notified if they can view the page. To choose which of these emails you get, see [Email notifications](/user-guide/preferences#email-notifications).
